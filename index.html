Utilized AI model workflows to normalise 132 raw banking/gateway error codes into 35 unique, structured error codes with complete classification based on business declines, technical declines, and customer declines.

Designed an automated end-to-end Freshdesk workflow. AI reads incoming merchant support tickets, extracts payment mode, error codes, and business impact, categorizes issues (UPI, Cards, Netbanking, API, Latency), and executes automated routing rules.

Impact: Reduced manual triage time, reassignment rate, and time-to-first-response while providing assigned team members with instant summaries and troubleshooting checklists.
